# Day 7 – AI-Assisted Excel: Finding Patterns in Sales Data

## Learning Objective
Practice identifying patterns in monthly sales data and using ChatGPT inside Excel to generate a structured analysis of trends, comparisons, unusual changes, and business recommendations.

## Dataset

| Month | Product | Sales |
|---|---|---:|
| January | Laptop | ₹45,000 |
| January | Mobile | ₹30,000 |
| February | Laptop | ₹50,000 |
| February | Mobile | ₹35,000 |
| March | Laptop | ₹40,000 |
| March | Mobile | ₹45,000 |
| April | Laptop | ₹55,000 |
| April | Mobile | ₹50,000 |
| May | Laptop | ₹60,000 |
| May | Mobile | ₹40,000 |

## Initial Observations

- Laptop had the highest sales in May at ₹60,000.
- Laptop sales increased overall from January to May, with a temporary dip in March.
- Mobile sales rose from January through April, then declined in May.
- Laptop sales were higher than Mobile sales in four of the five months; Mobile led in March.

## AI-Assisted Analysis

Using ChatGPT inside Excel, I generated a structured analysis of the monthly sales data.

- Laptop total sales: ₹2,50,000.
- Mobile total sales: ₹2,00,000.
- Laptop sales fell from ₹50,000 in February to ₹40,000 in March, a 20% decline.
- Mobile sales fell from ₹50,000 in April to ₹40,000 in May, a 20% decline.
- Sales values alone do not prove customer demand; units sold, prices, promotions, inventory, and competitor activity were not provided.

## Business Recommendations Suggested by AI

1. Investigate the March Laptop decline and May Mobile decline.
2. Review pricing, promotions, inventory, and competitor activity before drawing conclusions.
3. Maintain Laptop sales momentum while testing targeted Mobile promotions or bundles.

## How I Used AI

Used ChatGPT inside Excel to generate a structured analysis of the monthly sales data. Reviewed the AI-generated findings and discussed them to understand patterns, unusual changes, assumptions, and possible business actions.

## Key Takeaways

- AI can help summarize trends and comparisons directly within Excel.
- A sales increase or decrease is an observation; its cause requires further evidence.
- Sales value is not the same as demand or units sold.
- AI-generated analysis should be reviewed and validated rather than accepted without checking.

## Reflection

Today I practiced using ChatGPT inside Excel to analyze monthly sales data. I learned to review AI-generated trends, distinguish observations from assumptions, and consider what additional information is needed before making business recommendations.
