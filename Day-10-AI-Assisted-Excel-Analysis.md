
# Day 10 – AI-Assisted Excel Data Analysis

## Objective
Learn how to use AI for sales data analysis, identify business patterns, generate insights, and validate AI-generated results using Excel.

## Topics Covered
- AI prompt engineering for data analysis
- Product-wise sales analysis
- City-wise sales analysis
- Identifying top-performing products
- Identifying top-performing cities
- Calculating sales contribution percentages
- Validating AI-generated calculations and insights

## Practical Exercise

Analysed a sample sales dataset containing 10 records across three products and three cities.

Used AI to:
1. Calculate total sales by product.
2. Calculate total sales by city.
3. Identify the highest-selling product.
4. Identify the highest-performing city.
5. Generate business insights.
6. Suggest business recommendations.

## Results

### Product-wise Sales
- Laptop: ₹3,10,000
- Mobile: ₹1,20,000
- Tablet: ₹75,000

Highest-selling product: Laptop.

Laptop contributed 61.4% of total sales.

### City-wise Sales
- Bengaluru: ₹2,35,000
- Chennai: ₹1,35,000
- Hyderabad: ₹1,35,000

Highest-performing city: Bengaluru.

Bengaluru contributed 46.5% of total sales.

### Business Insights
1. Laptop is the top product, contributing 61.4% of total sales.
2. Bengaluru is the leading city, contributing 46.5% of total sales.
3. Chennai and Hyderabad generated equal sales of ₹1,35,000 each.

## Excel Validation

Used SUMIF to independently validate AI-generated calculations.

Product-wise calculation:
=SUMIF(A2:A11,"Laptop",C2:C11)

City-wise calculation:
=SUMIF(B2:B11,"Bengaluru",C2:C11)

Validation result: Excel calculations matched AI-generated results.

## Key Learnings
1. Structured prompts help AI understand analytical requirements.
2. AI can calculate sales figures and identify business patterns.
3. Excel formulas help independently verify AI-generated calculations.
4. Percentages help explain the contribution of products and cities.
5. AI-generated insights and recommendations should be validated before making business decisions.

## Takeaway
Today's practice helped me understand how AI can support practical sales data analysis.

I learned that getting answers from AI is not enough. A Data Analyst must also verify calculations, interpret insights, and evaluate recommendations before presenting findings.
