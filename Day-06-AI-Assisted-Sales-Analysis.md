# Day 6 – AI-Assisted Sales Analysis

## Learning Objective
Practice analyzing sales against targets, identifying underperforming product-city combinations, and using AI to validate and improve business recommendations.

## Topics Practiced
- Comparing actual sales with targets
- Identifying records below target
- Calculating sales gaps
- Summarizing sales by city and product
- Using AI to validate findings and refine business insights

## Dataset

| Product | City | Sales | Target |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Laptop | Bengaluru | 45,000 | 40,000 |
| Mobile | Chennai | 30,000 | 35,000 |
| Tablet | Hyderabad | 25,000 | 20,000 |
| Laptop | Chennai | 35,000 | 40,000 |
| Mobile | Bengaluru | 50,000 | 45,000 |
| Tablet | Hyderabad | 15,000 | 20,000 |

## Findings

- Chennai recorded sales of ₹65,000 against a target of ₹75,000, a shortfall of ₹10,000.
- Bengaluru recorded sales of ₹95,000 against a target of ₹85,000, exceeding the target by ₹10,000.
- Hyderabad recorded sales of ₹40,000 against a target of ₹40,000, meeting its overall target.
- Mobile–Chennai, Laptop–Chennai, and one Tablet–Hyderabad record were each ₹5,000 below their individual targets.
- Product totals across the dataset met their combined targets.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate Chennai's sales shortfall by reviewing customer demand, stock availability, salesperson activity, product visibility, and competitor activity.
2. Analyze Hyderabad's Tablet records to understand why performance differs across entries.
3. Consider targeted campaigns or pricing changes only after assessing profitability and customer behavior.
4. Avoid assuming discounts are the best solution, as they may reduce profit margins.

## Excel Skill

Practiced calculating the sales gap using:

`=C2-D2`

Assuming Sales is in column C and Target is in column D:
- Positive value: Above target
- Negative value: Below target
- Zero: Target met

## How I Used AI

Used AI to validate my initial analysis, clarify city-level versus individual-record performance, identify additional factors to investigate, and refine business recommendations.

## Key Takeaways

- Analyze both individual records and aggregated totals to avoid misleading conclusions.
- AI can help validate findings and broaden business questions.
- Business recommendations should consider profitability, not sales volume alone.
- Verify AI-generated conclusions against the source data.
