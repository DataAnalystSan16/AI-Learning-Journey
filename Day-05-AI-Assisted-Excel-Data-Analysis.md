📚 Key Takeaways

Sorting helps rank data and identify top performers.

Filtering helps focus on selected records or categories.

Comparing city totals can reveal differences in sales performance.

Business recommendations should consider more than total sales, including profitability and growth.

AI can guide analysis, while the analyst must validate results and apply business judgment.
