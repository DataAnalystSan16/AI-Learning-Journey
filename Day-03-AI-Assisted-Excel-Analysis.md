🧠 What I Practiced

1. Identifying Target Achievement

Used an IF formula to determine whether each product achieved its sales target.

=IF(B2>=C2,"ACHIEVED","NOT ACHIEVED")

Learning: Sales equal to or greater than the target are considered achieved.

2. Calculating Total Sales and Target

Used the SUM function to calculate the combined sales and target.

=SUM(B2:B6)

=SUM(C2:C6)

Results:

Total Sales: 185,000

Total Target: 185,000

Overall Difference: 0

The overall sales target was achieved exactly.

💡 Business Insights

Three products exceeded their targets.

Two products fell short of their targets.

Positive differences totaled 15,000.

Negative differences totaled -15,000.

Overall sales matched the combined target.

Even though the overall target was achieved, Mobile and Monitor require attention.

A Data Analyst should investigate the reasons for their sales shortfalls and review profit margins before concluding how much these shortfalls affected profitability.
